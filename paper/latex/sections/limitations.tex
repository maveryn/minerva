\section{Limitations and Future Work}
\label{sec:limitations}

Our study has several limitations. \textbf{Uncertainty and training variation.} Our paired bootstrap confidence intervals capture variation over evaluation examples for fixed checkpoints, not variation across independent training seeds. Multi-seed RLVR experiments would provide a stronger estimate of training stability but were not computationally feasible across four post-trained backbones. \textbf{Compute and ablations.} Because RLVR training is expensive, the component controls in \Cref{tab:eval12-ablation-summary} are limited to Llama-3.1-8B, and we sweep only the distillation learning-rate scale $\gamma$. Broader backbone-level ablations and hyperparameter sweeps, including the distillation interval $I$, per-interval cap $M$, and EMA decay $\alpha$, remain future work. The current experiments evaluate the complete hard-prompt allocation policy but do not isolate the gate from always-on, random-prompt, or strict-zero-only ACR allocation. These alternatives change the eligible prompt pool: holding the $M=256$ distillation cap fixed changes buffer composition or fill rate, whereas matching accepted-trace exposure may require different ACR-generation compute or update timing. \textbf{Data coverage.} Minerva-CTI and our evaluation suite are primarily English-centric; multilingual CTI, regional reporting styles, and non-English security artifacts remain important extensions. \textbf{Trace filtering.} MinervaRL uses lightweight heuristics and a TextCNN filter for ACR trace selection. Stronger LLM-based filters may improve trace quality but would increase cost, motivating more scalable quality estimators. \textbf{Training overhead.} MinervaRL adds overhead from ACR generation, log-probability computation, and periodic distillation. \hyperref[app:timing-overhead]{Appendix~\ref*{app:timing-overhead}} reports a 39.3\% increase in a 10-step Llama-3.1-8B timing study, although \Cref{fig:validation-accuracy-dynamics} shows faster progress toward strong validation performance. Reducing this overhead while preserving finite-budget performance is an important direction.
