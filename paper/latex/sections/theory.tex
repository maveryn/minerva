\subsection{A Stylized Finite-Budget Detectability View}
\label{sec:minervarl-support-bridging}

Let $a^\star$ denote the ground-truth structured target for prompt $x$. Because Minerva's task verifiers include extraction, normalization, alias handling, structured partial credit, and set-valued scoring, we define full success through the verifier itself:
\begin{equation}
    S(x,y;a^\star)
    :=
    \mathbb{1}\!\left\{R_{\textsc{minerva}}(x,y,a^\star)=1\right\},
    \qquad
    p_\theta(a^\star\mid x)
    :=
    \Pr_{y\sim\pi_\theta(\cdot\mid x)}[S(x,y;a^\star)=1].
\end{equation}
With $k$ independent rollouts, the probability that the rollout group contains no fully verified completion is
\begin{equation}
    \Pr[\text{no full success in $k$ rollouts}]
    =
    \left(1-p_\theta(a^\star\mid x)\right)^k .
\end{equation}
For a failure tolerance $\zeta\in(0,1)$, define the detectability threshold
\begin{equation}
    \varepsilon_{k,\zeta}
    :=
    1-\zeta^{1/k}
    \approx
    \frac{-\log \zeta}{k}.
\end{equation}
When $p_\theta(a^\star\mid x)\ge\varepsilon_{k,\zeta}$, the probability of missing a fully verified completion in $k$ rollouts is at most $\zeta$. Conversely, below this threshold, full success is not reliably observed under the available rollout budget.

The no-full-success event $m_i=\max_j r_{i,j}<1$ is the condition used by the ACR gate.

For a gated prompt, the answer-conditioned prompt $x^{\mathrm{acr}}=x\oplus b(a^\star,d)$ can produce a fully verified trace, which is filtered and distilled back onto the original prompt $x$. This step aims to raise $p_\theta(a^\star\mid x)$ above the finite-budget detectability threshold. Appendix~\ref{app:minervarl-support-bridging} states a conditional finite-sampling formalization: if accepted ACR traces occur with nonzero probability and each successful original-prompt distillation update increases the log success probability by a fixed amount, repeated updates cross the threshold in finite expected time. These assumptions posit the effect of distillation rather than derive it from joint GRPO/SFT optimization, so the result is a stylized conditional argument, not an optimization guarantee or independent evidence for the method. It also assumes $p_{\theta_0}(a^\star\mid x)>0$ and therefore concerns finite-budget detectability rather than expansion of mathematical support.
